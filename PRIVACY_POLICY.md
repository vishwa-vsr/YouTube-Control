# Privacy Policy for YouTube Control: Shorts Blocker & Detox

**Effective Date:** July 29, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 29, 2026

**YouTube Control: Shorts Blocker & Detox** ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data and information.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection
We respect your privacy. **YouTube Control does NOT collect, track, transmit, or store any personal data, browsing history, analytics, or user activity.**

* We do **not** collect your name, email address, IP address, or location.
* We do **not** track which videos you watch, search queries you make, or channels you visit.
* We do **not** use any analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) or third-party tracking scripts.

---

## 2. Local Storage Only
All user preferences (such as enabling thumbnail blurring, custom grid column selections, or sidebar toggle options) are stored **100% locally** on your device using the browser's native `chrome.storage.local` API.

* Your settings never leave your browser.
* No data is ever uploaded to external servers or cloud services.

---

## 3. Host Permissions
The Extension requests permission to run on `*.youtube.com` strictly to apply your custom layout preferences, modify visual styles, and block Shorts elements on YouTube. It does not access or modify data on any other website.

---

## 4. Third-Party Sharing
Because we do not collect any data, **we do not sell, trade, rent, or share any user information** with third parties under any circumstances.

---
